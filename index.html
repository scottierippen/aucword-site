Questions

Frequently asked

Does Aucword work with my existing eBay watchlist?
Yes. Sign in with eBay once and the watchlist you already have imports automatically. There is nothing to re-add by hand.
Will Aucword bid for me?
No. Aucword never places a bid. It tracks your auctions and tells you when to act — you place the bid on eBay yourself.
Do I need to give Aucword my eBay password?
No. Signing in goes through eBay's own OAuth flow, so your password is entered on eBay's site and never reaches Aucword.
What alerts can I get before an auction ends?
You can set a price target per item and get a push notification when an auction is closing in or reaches the price you set. Alerts can fire once or repeat.
Does it work when the app is closed?
Yes. Alerts arrive as push notifications, and the home screen widget and Lock Screen countdown keep updating in the background.
Is Aucword free?
Yes. Aucword is free to download and use, with no subscription.
Does it work for anything besides sports cards?
Yes. Aucword works with any eBay auction on your watchlist. It's built with card and collectibles buyers in mind, but nothing is category-specific.
When is the Android version coming out?
An Android version is in active development. Follow @aucword and we'll post the day it lands.
How do I delete my account?
Open Settings in the app and choose Delete Account. Full instructions, including what gets deleted, are on the account deletion page.
