Closed beta · limited spots

Aucword for Android is open for testing.

The same live auction countdowns, price-target alerts, and home screen widget that Aucword runs on iOS — now on Android, ahead of the public release. Getting in takes about two minutes and three taps.

Do this on your Android phone if you can. The Google account you use below has to be the same one signed into the Play Store on the device you want the app on — doing it on a laptop signed into a different account is the one mistake that trips almost everyone up.

How to join

1

Join the tester group

Google Play only shows a closed beta to accounts on an approved list, and this group is that list. Tap through and choose Join group.

Join the Google Group

Sign in with the Google account that's on your phone.

2

Opt in to the test

Once you're in the group, this page turns your account into a tester. Tap Become a tester.

Become a tester

If it says you're not eligible, give step 1 a few minutes to propagate and reload.

3

Install from Google Play

The opt-in page gives you a download link straight to the Play Store listing. Install it like any other app — updates arrive automatically.

Newly opted-in accounts sometimes wait a few hours before the listing appears. If Play says the app isn't available yet, it usually is by the next time you look.

What you're getting

Everything the iOS app does, on Android:

  • Live countdowns on every auction in your eBay watchlist, colour-coded by urgency
  • Price-target alerts as push notifications before an auction closes
  • A home screen widget showing what's ending soonest, with the current bid
  • Automatic import of the eBay watchlist you already have — nothing to re-add by hand

It's a beta, so expect the occasional rough edge. Aucword still never places a bid and never sees your eBay password.

Found a bug? Tell us

Beta feedback goes straight to the person who can fix it. Email hi@aucword.com with your phone model, Android version, and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help enormously.

Staying opted in for the duration of the test is what gets Aucword to a public Android release — Google requires a sustained group of active testers before the app can graduate to the open Play Store. If you join, please keep it installed.

Troubleshooting

Why do I have to join a Google Group first?
Google Play's closed testing track only shows the app to accounts on an approved tester list, and a Google Group is how Play manages that list. Joining the group is what puts your Google account on it. We don't post to the group and it isn't a mailing list — it exists purely as the permission check.
Play says the app isn't available for my account
  • Check which Google account is signed into the Play Store on your phone — tap your avatar, top right. It has to match the account that joined the group.
  • If you have several Google accounts, join the group again with the one that's actually on the phone.
  • Give it time. Opt-ins can take anywhere from a few minutes to a few hours to reach the Play Store.
  • Still stuck after a day? Email hi@aucword.com with the Google account address and we'll add you to the list by hand.
Is the beta free?
Yes. Free, no subscription, same as on iOS. You'll need an eBay account, connected through eBay's own sign-in.
Will my account work on both iOS and Android?
Yes. Sign in with the same Aucword account and your eBay connection, watchlist, and price targets follow you across both.
How do I leave the beta?
Open the opt-in link again and choose "Leave the program", then uninstall the app. You can leave the Google Group whenever you like too. If you'd also like your Aucword account deleted, the account deletion page covers that.
When does Android get a public release?
As soon as the closed test clears Google's requirements and the build is stable. Follow @aucword on X, Instagram, or TikTok and we'll post the day it lands.

On an iPhone instead?

Aucword is out now on the App Store — download it free, no beta required.
