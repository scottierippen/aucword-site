Aucword support

Aucword for iOS · Last updated: July 2026

Most problems are one of the five below — watchlist sync, auction alerts, widget refresh, the eBay connection, or signing in. If none of them match, email hi@aucword.com and a real person will read it. Include your device, iOS version, and what you expected to happen; it gets us to an answer much faster.

Common fixes — watchlist, alerts, and widgets

My watchlist is empty, or items are missing
  • Open the app and give it a moment — Aucword syncs your watchlist from eBay each time you come back to it.
  • Auctions that have already ended drop off the watchlist. That's eBay's behaviour, not a sync failure.
  • If it's still empty, open Settings and check the eBay connection status. Reconnecting re-authorises Aucword to read your watchlist.
I'm not getting notifications
  • Check that notifications are allowed: iOS Settings → Aucword → Notifications.
  • Alerts are per item. Tap the bell on an auction and confirm an alert is actually set and saved.
  • Check whether a Focus mode or Do Not Disturb schedule is suppressing delivery.
  • Still nothing? Email us with the item and the alert you set and we'll look at the delivery logs for your account.
The widget is showing old information
  • iOS decides how often widgets are allowed to refresh, so a widget can lag behind the app by a few minutes.
  • Opening Aucword refreshes the data the widget reads from.
  • If it's stuck for much longer than that, remove the widget and add it again.
It says my eBay account isn't connected
  • eBay authorisations expire, and they can also be revoked from your eBay account settings.
  • Open Settings in Aucword and reconnect. You'll be taken to eBay's own sign-in page — Aucword never sees your eBay password.
I can't sign in to Aucword
  • Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen to get a reset link by email.
  • Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
  • Note there are two separate logins: your Aucword account, and your eBay account that Aucword connects to.
Is there an Android version?
Aucword for Android is in closed testing on Google Play and open to join — the Android beta page has the two steps. A public release follows once the closed test wraps up; follow @aucword on X, Instagram, or TikTok and we'll post the day it lands.
